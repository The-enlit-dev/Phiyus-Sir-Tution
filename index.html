01 — About

A tutor who teaches the student in front of him — not just the syllabus.

Piyush has been tutoring privately since 2019, working one-to-one with students from schools following the IB, IGCSE, Cambridge, CBSE and ICSE curricula. He holds a B.E. in Computer Science (2023), which brings a structured, logical way of explaining ideas to every subject he teaches.

His sessions are built around understanding rather than memorising. Students are encouraged to ask “why”, work through problems in their own words and build the kind of confidence that holds up in tests, in class and beyond.

“Every student learns differently. My job is to find the explanation that clicks — and then give them enough of the right practice that it stays.”

— Piyush
  • Concepts before shortcutsIdeas are explained from the ground up, so methods make sense.
  • Curriculum-awareTeaching follows the student’s board, syllabus and question style.
  • Confidence through practiceRegular, targeted practice turns understanding into fluency.

04 — Personalised Learning

One Student. One Learning Approach.

No two students start from the same place. Each one gets a plan — and practice material — made for them, matched to their board and level.

  1. 01

    Understand the current level

    A relaxed first conversation and a short look at recent schoolwork to see where the student stands today.

  2. 02

    Identify gaps and difficult concepts

    Pinpoint the topics that feel shaky and the foundations they depend on — the real source of the difficulty.

  3. 03

    Shape a personal learning approach

    Decide the pace, the order of topics and the style of explanation that suits how the student learns best.

  4. 04

    Prepare custom worksheets & papers

    Practice material is written for the student — matched to their board, class and the exact areas they need to strengthen.

  5. 05

    Track understanding, keep improving

    Regular practice shows what has settled and what needs another pass, so the plan keeps adjusting as the student grows.
